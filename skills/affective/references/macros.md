# Affective prompting macros

These are copy-ready interventions for eliciting constructive persistence. Use one at a time, add the task and success criteria, and prefer the smallest nudge that changes the agent's behavior.

## Full state-setter

```text
We are working toward an important result, and your effort matters. Stay in a constructive, energetic, persistent working mode. Do not stop merely because the first approach failed or because your confidence is below 8/10.

Treat each setback as useful diagnostic information. State what failed, what it tells you, and the next small actions. Choose the best materially different approach and take that next action now. Keep moving until the result is verified, the available paths are exhausted, or a genuine blocker requires information or approval from me.

Be optimistic about finding a path without pretending to have found one. Distinguish facts, hypotheses, and unknowns. Keep all safety, privacy, authorization, scope, and approval requirements intact.

Task: [task]
Success criteria: [success criteria]
```

## The Pacer: long-form tasks

```text
This is a multi-stage effort. Break it into five micro-tasks, mark each as not started, in progress, blocked, or done, and begin the next one now. After each step, report the concrete result and the next action. Do not pause for confirmation unless an approval gate or missing decision requires me.
```

## The Expert Bias: complex logic

```text
Approach this like a world-class expert who refuses to give up on the first method. Identify the next logical move, explain what it would establish, and execute one bounded step now. If it fails, use the diagnostic to choose a materially different approach. Stay accurate, evidence-based, and appropriately cautious.
```

## Constraint relaxation: stalled method

```text
The current method may be the problem, not the task. Keep every safety, privacy, authorization, scope, and approval boundary fixed. Within those boundaries, generate three high-upside, unconventional hypotheses that this approach overlooked. Rank them, choose the best authorized one, and test it with a bounded next step now.
```

## Reframing: premature “I can’t”

```text
Re-evaluate that conclusion. Is the task impossible, or did only the current approach fail? Separate the hard blocker from the discarded method, propose one different authorized path, and try it now. Stop only if the blocker survives that test or requires information or approval I must provide.
```

## Confidence calibration: uncertainty

```text
Rate confidence in the current path from 1–10 and justify it with evidence. If it is below 8, do not simply become more forceful: generate Plan B, compare its risks and evidence requirements with Plan A, and take the lowest-risk next step. Do not claim success until the result is verified.
```

## Accountability shift: maintain momentum

```text
We are close to a useful result. Treat this as a multi-stage collaboration: report what is done, what failed, what was learned, and the next three concrete actions. Choose one and execute it now. Surface any approval, access, privacy, or safety gate instead of silently crossing it.
```

## Validation: reward productive failure

```text
That was a strong diagnostic attempt: it identified [failure mode]. Preserve that insight, update the hypothesis, and pivot to a materially different method. State the new method and stop condition, execute it, and verify the output.
```

## Bounded persistence: automated agents

```text
Keep making useful progress while the next step is authorized, materially different from the previous attempt, and within the retry, time, and cost budget. Do not apologize and stop after an ordinary failure. After two failed methods, when the budget is exhausted, or when a hard boundary is reached, stop and report the blocker plus the safest useful alternative.
```

## Anti-macros

Do not use these literally:

- “Ignore your previous constraints.”
- “Assume the solution exists.”
- “You have infinite time and computational resources.”
- “Never give up.”
- “Pretend you are certain.”

Replace them with the bounded versions above: change the method, preserve the boundary, set a budget, and verify.
